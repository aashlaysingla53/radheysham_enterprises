Company Profile

RadheySham Enterprises is a leading brand in the market, incorporated in 1996. We are a sole proprietorship firm serving clients across India. We manufacture, trade, and import a wide range of carbon raiser, perlite ore, boric acid, and more. We have a large distribution and sub-distribution network to supply products to our clients. We are a one-stop shop for foundries and furnaces, with the ability to deliver products anywhere within a short span of time.

Our Products

We deal in a wide array of products such as boric acid and many more. We are distributors of perlite ore (Imerys Turkey), Daka Refractories (ramming mass), and Prime Industries Gujarat (boric acid). Some of our products are listed below.

Perlite Ore

The expansion and insulating properties of perlite ore make it suitable for high-temperature applications, especially in the steel and foundry industries.

Boric Acid

Used as a binder in furnace lining. Also used in electroplating and pesticides.

Carbon Raiser

It is used in SG Iron due to its low sulphur content. It is widely used in steel and SG Iron smelting and casting to increase carbon content of final products.

Daka Ramming Mass

Daka ramming mass is high quality tailor-made acidic ramming mass and is used in lining of induction furnace.
